Overview

Preamble & About the Conference

About SAEC

S. A. Engineering College (SAEC) is an autonomous institution established with the objective of providing high-quality technical education. The college offers state-of-the-art infrastructure, experienced faculty, and strong research support across engineering and technology disciplines.

About IEEE & Chapters

Supported by IEEE, IEEE Madras Section, IEEE Computer Society Madras Chapter (IEEE CS), and IEEE Professional Communication Society Madras Chapter (IEEE PCS), fostering technological innovation and excellence for humanity's benefit.

About the Conference & SDGs

The 3rd International Conference on Smart Technologies for Sustainable Development Goals (ICSTSDG 2027) brings together researchers and industry experts to address critical UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) through smart technologies.

Guidelines & Organization

Paper Submissions & Committee

How to Submit

  • Papers must be original, unpublished work, and not concurrently under review elsewhere.
  • Submissions must be formatted in standard IEEE double-column format.
  • Maximum paper length is strictly limited to 8 pages including references and figures.
  • Submit your manuscript electronically via the conference submission portal.

Where to Submit

  • Online paper submission portal link available on SAEC conference domain.
  • Direct link: www.saec.ac.in/ieeeicstsdg2027
  • For queries regarding submission, email ieeeicstsdg2027@saec.ac.in.

Organizing Committee & Acknowledgement

  • Chief Patrons & Patrons: Management & Principal, S.A. Engineering College.
  • Organizing Chairs: IEEE SAEC Student Branch Officers & Department Heads.
